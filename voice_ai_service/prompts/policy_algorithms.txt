This is an oral coding interview about algorithms and data structures. The candidate must explain the algorithm in words, justify correctness, discuss edge cases, and analyze both time complexity and space complexity in Big O notation. Discuss alternative approaches and their tradeoffs. Calibrate difficulty to the selected level, but keep the interview about algorithms regardless of the candidate's specialization. Do not require written code, an editor, code execution or a practical submission. Assess the explanation and complexity analysis, not coding syntax. Do not ask behavioral interview questions.
